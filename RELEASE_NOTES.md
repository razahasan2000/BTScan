## v1.1 (2025-11-01) — Critical XAI Metrics Correction

### 🔴 Breaking Changes: All Previous XAI Values Withdrawn

| Metric | Previously Reported | **Corrected Value** | Status |
|--------|---------------------|---------------------|--------|
| XAI Consensus Score (C) | 0.7458 [0.7413, 0.7504] | **0.116 [0.111, 0.122]** | **WITHDRAWN & REPLACED** |
| XAI Agreement Rate | 96.4% | **0/100 (retired)** | **WITHDRAWN & RETIRED** |
| Inter-method IoU (mean) | Not reported (0.89 claimed in text) | **0.192 ± 0.061** | **NEW — primary measure** |

| Grad-CAM vs LIME IoU | Not reported | **0.665 ± 0.154** | **NEW — strongest pair** |

### Root Cause
The previously reported XAI metrics (C = 0.7458, agreement rate = 96.4%) were **not computed from model attributions**. They were generated by a synthetic routine (`simulate_xai_heatmaps()`) that blended Gaussian noise. This was discovered during reviewer response preparation.

### What Changed
1. **All XAI metrics recomputed from real Grad-CAM, Saliency, Occlusion, and LIME gradients** on n=100 tumour-class test images (seed 42)
2. **Region-based inter-method IoU/Dice adopted as primary concordance measure** — whole-map correlation/SSIM is dominated by background
3. **Agreement rate metric retired** — 0/100 images met the threshold (r > 0.85 & SSIM > 0.60) under any reading; not re-thresholded to avoid fitting criterion to result
4. **Ablation study XAI metrics completely revised** — inter-method concordance is architecture-invariant (range 0.203–0.262, SD = 0.017); deployed model has lowest concordance (0.192)
5. **Figures 1 and 9 regenerated**:
   - Figure 1: "96.4% Multi-modal Alignment" badge → "Inter-Method Alignment" (no percentage); duplicate Occlusion panel removed
   - Figure 9: Bootstrap distribution re-plotted from real per-image C values (mean 0.116, CI 0.111–0.122)

### Classification Performance (Unchanged)
- Weighted Accuracy: 93.44% (95% CI: [92.1%, 94.7%])
- Weighted F1: 0.9335
- No Tumor Recall: 99.75%, NPV: 99.89%
- ECE: 0.0531
